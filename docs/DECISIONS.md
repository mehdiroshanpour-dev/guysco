# Owner decisions during build

- Dashboard hero banner: use a render of our own 3D office scene (not a stock photo). Until stage 3 exists, stage 2 uses a temporary placeholder; after stage 3 the banner is captured from the scene and replaced.
- Team size: 4 members (the 8 people in the dashboard reference image are illustrative only).
- Stage 2 includes Board, List and Timeline views, plus Calendar and Files pages.
- Office map: elements with no function are removed (e.g. the top notch/window between the two upper offices and the curved desk behind the conference room).
- Characters: the 20 in `assets/characters.json` are approved (Cat included).
- Emotes: shown as a bubble above each character's head (no face plane on the model).
- Sitting for characters without a sit animation: posed in code; flying characters hover above the chair.
- Source zips stay in the repo but are excluded from the site (`.vercelignore`; only `public/models/` is served).
- Office door rules for visitors: locked or frosted → closed; private → only members with an accepted private-chat invite; public → open, view only (only the owner sits at the desk).
- Talking without a mouth: the head nods and the arms move with the voice level (owner decision); emotes show in a bubble above the head.
- Furniture: Kenney Furniture Kit (CC0) from a GitHub mirror (github.com/ETdoFresh/kenney.nl); walls, glass, doors, floor lines are built in code.
- Office fixes (owner request): building and conference room enlarged (corridor half width 7 m, offices 8×8 m, conference radius 4.5 m); compact reception desk with a chair, the receptionist sits and types; her tour walks to each area (reception, floor lines, conference room, office, desk/dashboard, lounge) with the speech bubble above her head; E next to her replays it.
- Doors (offices, conference room, entrance) open and close with E; state is shared over the WebRTC events channel (newest command wins). Visitors without access always see an office door closed. Glass porch outside the entrance.
- Characters, chairs, plants and door leaves block walking. A taken chair cannot be used by a second person.
- At a desk monitor only the Fullscreen and Exit buttons are shown; in fullscreen they sit in a 48 px bar above the dashboard so nothing covers it.
- Computers: Tab switches between mouse look and a brand-styled cursor. Conference/lounge chairs allow free looking around.
- Phones in landscape (height ≤ 500 px, `short:` variant): side menu instead of the bottom bar, compact office UI.
- Nameplates and speech bubbles in the 3D office (owner reference images): dark green pill with a cream rim, a gold-ringed badge with rays and sparkles, and a status dot with a line under the name (the line follows the voice level; the receptionist's moves while she types). The badge shows a crown for the receptionist and the person's portrait for members. Speech bubbles (tour and teammates' messages) are frosted glass with a teal sparkle badge, the speaker's portrait, a counter chip (tour step, or message time / history position), a round dark-green arrow button and a dotted glowing tail to the head. The tour keeps a small "Skip" link.
- Conference room after the owner's reference image (built in code): a round light-oak table (radius 1.75 m) with a planter in the middle and a glowing ring under its pedestal, 8 light-blue armchairs with white frames, a tablet ("Steam Deck") at every seat, a large "Team Meeting" screen (Raise Hand, Share Idea, Share Image, Share Screen, More; Persian or English with the page) above an oak media cabinet with a camera, palms on both sides, a rose carpet and a ring of light in the ceiling. Behind the screen the ring is cream panelling with dark posts instead of glass (about 120°); the rest stays glass. The screen and tablet buttons are pictures for now (what they should do is still open).
- 3D lighting and quality: soft image-based light built from glowing panels (no download), lit ceilings, light walls brighter towards the top, soft contact shadows under furniture and people, glowing lamps. High quality (computers by default) adds ambient occlusion, a light glow (bloom) and a vignette, loaded only when used; phones in low quality skip these effects.
- Office owner panel (owner request): closed by default. In one's own office the place name in the top bar is a small button (settings icon, name, chevron, plus icons for frosted glass, lock and focus while they are on); the panel opens below it and its close button folds it back. It closes again on leaving the office.
- Personal to-dos (owner request), a card on the home dashboard, private to each member (table `todos`, read-own RLS, live on the member's devices): smart quick add that reads the day (today, tomorrow, day after, weekdays, next week; Persian and English), `!`/`!!`/"urgent" for priority and `*`/"important" for a star, with a live preview; due chips (overdue in red), star and priority per item, options for today/tomorrow/a date/no date/priority/delete, tap to edit; filters All/Today/Starred; progress bar with an emoji, confetti and a short cheer on each completion (a bigger burst when all are done, a light vibration on phones); a streak of days in a row with something done. Cleared done items are kept 60 days for the streak and then removed by the daily cleanup.
- Conference room smart wall panel (owner request): a panel on the glass right of the door (inside). E (computers) or the "Light & glass" button (phones) opens its controls: light scenes (Meeting, Present, Relax, Off), brightness and warm/cool sliders, and the glass frosting (Clear, Half, Frosted, or any level). The light changes the ceiling light, ring and downlights, the glow under the table and, for people inside, the general light of the room; the screen and tablets keep their own light. Frosting fades every pane of the ring and the glass door. Any member may change it; changes reach everyone at once over WebRTC (newest wins) and are saved in the `rooms` table so late arrivals see the same room.
- Project command center (owner request), one page per project (`/projects/[id]`, opened from its card): status (planning, active, paused, done) and a deadline per project; a health badge (on track, at risk, off track) worked out from open critical bugs, the deadline, late milestones, overdue tasks and progress against the elapsed time, with its reasons shown. Overview: progress, open bugs, ideas to review and the next milestone; a "needs attention" list; quick actions (report a bug, add an idea, the project's tasks, copy a progress report); a 14-day trend of tasks finished and bugs fixed; task status and team workload charts. Bugs and defects (`issues`, `issue_comments`): severity, steps to reproduce, assignee, up to 6 screenshots (choose, drop or paste), status Open/In progress/Fixed/Closed, filters and search, a discussion thread and a full-size image viewer; the assignee is notified and the project manager too for a critical bug. Ideas and upgrades (`project_ideas`, `idea_votes`): anyone suggests and votes; the manager (or an admin) sets the status and turns one into a task on the board. Milestones (`milestones`) on a timeline with a today marker and a task timeline; managed by the project manager or an admin. All tables are member-readable and live.
- Save feedback (owner report: settings, avatar and other save buttons "do not work well"): every save button shows "Saving…" while it runs, a short "Saved ✓" after, and "Not saved; tap again" when it fails (settings, task, event, project, role, poll and account forms). The character and status change at once everywhere (avatar, office) and roll back if the save fails. Changing the password from settings no longer jumps to the home page; only the forced first-login change continues to the app.
- Phone bottom bar: a soft pill glides to the tapped item at once (before the page loads) and its icon springs up; chat and menu give a short pop. Respects reduced motion.
- 3D (owner request): the wall above every door is closed (glass transom over glass doors, frosted with its office; a wall piece over the entrance). The receptionist sits closer to the desk with her hands on the keyboard, and her monitor stands straight in front of her face. Conference room plants are now slim sage-green blades in small white pots, and a low moss bed in the table (not eye-catching).
- 3D look (owner reference image: clean, glassy, calm on the eyes): ceiling downlights are soft cones towards the floor (no bright spot on the ceiling), smaller lamp discs with a rim, a polished floor with a light clear coat that picks up soft reflections, clearer glass, smoother furniture, neutral tone mapping, gentler glow and vignette. Phones now start in high quality too (lighter ambient-occlusion setting); only if a phone stays too slow at the lowest render scale does the office switch to low quality by itself (the gauge button switches back). Low quality got a soft key light so it is no longer flat.
- Drawing board (owner request: draw, write and add pictures on the conference Steam Deck, show it on the big screen, draw from the phone): every member has their own board (16:9, the screen's shape) with pen, eraser, text (Persian or English), pictures (chosen or pasted; scaled down to 1600 px and uploaded like other files, so they also appear in Files), move, six colours, three sizes, undo/redo and clear. It opens from the "Drawing board" button when seated at the conference table, and as its own page (`/board`, in the menu) so a phone can be the drawing pad while the computer is in the office: a member's board is the same on all their open devices. "Show on screen" puts it live on the conference screen for everyone; one presenter at a time (the first claim wins a race); it ends when the presenter stops or closes all their tabs. Others in the room get "View screen" to see it full size on their own device. Boards travel only over the WebRTC mesh (not stored in the database); each member's board is kept in their browser between visits.
- To-do game (owner request, inspired by the owner's reference images): finishing a personal to-do gives XP (10, +5 medium / +10 urgent, +5 starred, +5 done on or before its day), taken back if it is un-ticked; levels need 100, 200, 300… more XP each. The card shows a hexagon level badge, an XP bar with "+XP" rising from the ticked box, the streak flame and this week's days (Saturday first in Persian) with a tick on days with something done. Tabs: To-dos (filters All/Today/Starred and categories), Quests (three a day: finish 3 to-dos, finish an urgent or starred one, plan one for a later day; all three give a +25 XP bonus once a day) and Achievements (Finisher, On fire, Firefighter, Early bird before 9 am, Quest hero, Climber, Balanced; bronze, silver and gold with progress to the next tier; locked ones greyed). Level-ups, new badges and finished quests are celebrated with confetti and a line of text. Categories (Personal, Work, Learning, Health) are typed as #کار, #work, #یادگیری, #learn, #سلامتی, #health, #شخصی, #personal or picked in an item's options. The score lives on the server (`todo_stats`: XP and the achievement counters, read-own RLS; `todos.xp` remembers what each finished item gave) so cleaning up old to-dos never lowers it.
